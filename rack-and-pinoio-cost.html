What Is a Rack and Pinion Steering System?

A rack and pinion steering system is a mechanical device that transforms rotational movement from the steering wheel into linear movement to control the vehicle’s front wheels.

The system mainly consists of:

Steering rack、Pinion gear、Tie rods、Steering housing、Hydraulic or electric assist components (depending on the vehicle)

When the driver turns the steering wheel, the pinion gear rotates and moves the rack horizontally. This movement pushes or pulls the tie rods, allowing the wheels to turn.

Modern vehicles commonly use three types of steering racks:

1. Manual Steering Rack Manual steering racks do not use hydraulic or electric assistance. They are simple in design and usually have lower replacement costs.

2. Hydraulic Power Steering Rack Hydraulic steering racks use fluid pressure to assist steering. They provide smoother steering but contain more components, making repair and replacement more expensive.

3. Electric Power Steering Rack (EPS) Electric steering racks use electronic motors and sensors. They offer improved fuel efficiency and advanced vehicle control but generally have higher replacement costs.
